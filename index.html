Fleet Log

A cost-per-mile system for hot shot and Class 8 owner-operators. Log trips from a one-tap form on your phone; Google Maps returns the loaded and deadhead miles; the sheet gives you your true cost per mile and a GO / NO-GO verdict before you take the load.

  • Runs entirely in your own Google account
  • One payment, no subscription
  • Monday summary email and a one-click tax packet
